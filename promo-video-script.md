# NVYT Promotional Video Script

**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Tone:** Energetic & Modern
**Audience:** Event Organizers

---

## OPENING (0-5 sec)

**[Fast cuts of people at events - conferences, parties, community gatherings]**

**VO:** "You've got the vision. The venue. The vibe."

---

## THE PROBLEM (5-10 sec)

**[Show frustrated organizer with spreadsheets, manual lists, cash handling]**

**VO:** "But managing registrations, chasing payments, and tracking attendees? *That's* the hard part."

---

## INTRODUCING NVYT (10-15 sec)

**[NVYT logo reveal with energetic motion graphics]**

**VO:** "Meet NVYT — your all-in-one event platform built for Filipino organizers."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: nvyt.app]**

---

## FEATURE SHOWCASE (15-45 sec)

**[Quick screen recordings / animations of each feature]**

### Event Creation (15-22 sec)

**VO:** "Create stunning event pages in minutes. Custom designs. Your branding. Professional results."

### Ticketing & Payments (22-30 sec)

**VO:** "Sell tickets and get paid instantly. GCash. Maya. Cards. All the ways Filipinos pay — built right in."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: As low as 2% platform fee]**

### Attendee Management (30-37 sec)

**VO:** "Track every registration. Send announcements. Manage your guest list — all from one dashboard."

### Check-in (37-45 sec)

**VO:** "And on event day? Scan QR codes at the door. Fast. Seamless. No lines, no stress."

**[Show phone scanning QR, green checkmark animation]**

---

## CLOSING / CTA (45-60 sec)

**[Montage of happy event moments - people connecting, laughing, networking]**

**VO:** "From intimate dinners to sold-out conferences — NVYT has everything you need to bring people together."

**[NVYT logo + tagline]**

**VO:** "Make plans. Bring people."

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: Create your first event FREE at nvyt.app]**

**VO:** "Start for free at nvyt.app"

---

## Technical Notes

| Element | Suggestion |
|---------|------------|
| **Music** | Upbeat electronic/indie track, builds energy |
| **Pacing** | Quick cuts (1-2 sec each), modern editing style |
| **Visuals** | Mix of real event footage + clean UI screen captures |
| **Text** | Minimal on-screen text, let VO carry the message |
| **End card** | Logo + URL + "Create Your First Event Free" CTA |
